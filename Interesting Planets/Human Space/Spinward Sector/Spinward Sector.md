## Spinward Sector
> "The Spinward Sector, humanity's last great frontier. Better make the most of it: TerraGov ain't gonna ignore it forever, and when they do come down, they're going to come down hard."

Although the frontier has moved on, the Spinward Sector is arguably even more significant to humanity than ever before, and with this newfound importance comes new opportunities- and new challenges. Increased attention from both TerraGov and corporations, the collapse of the Third Soviet Union, the numerous Tiziran wars- the Spinward Sector is a locus of interest, with a number of suitors competing for influence over it.

The sector is sparsely populated, with the vast majority of the population being within the borders of the Spinward Stellar Coalition, an independent (for now) human state that claims dominion over most of the Sector's stars. TerraGov holds little sway in this region, but as the borders of humanity's main state continue to push ever-spinward, Manhattan's influence continues to grow. Within a few decades, it seems likely that the Spinward Sector will be firmly part of the Terran Federation.

### A Brief History of the Spinward Sector
Human history within the Spinward Sector begins with the scattered exploration efforts undertaken by wildcat explorers during the early days of FTL travel. The systems of the sector were first surveyed during the mid 2300s, although humanity was in no particular position to exploit this knowledge at the time. For the next century, the sector would be mostly ignored by humanity, with most colonial efforts by the Terran population looking trailward. Small wildcat mining operations and minor corporate expeditions would occasionally set up shop, only to be lost or abandoned within a few years. All of this changed, however, with the arrival of the Red Tomorrow Society, who selected the Spinward Sector as the home of their new communist utopia: the Third Soviet Union.

Under the Third Soviet Union, the Spinward Sector saw its first concerted efforts at settlement, with the generation ships brought by the Red Tomorrow Society fanning out over a number of habitable systems in the region and putting down permanent roots. As word spread of this newfound "utopia", an increasing number of settlers came to join the fledgeling state, and the Third Soviet Union grew into a true nation, with a sizeable population spread over a wide, but poorly developed, territory.

After several decades of peace, the idyllic dream of the Third Soviet Union unfortunately came to a crushing end, with mounting instability and an increasing autocratic streak in the Supreme Soviet and the Chairman leading to the outbreak of civil war with the February Revolution of 2505. The revolutionaries seize New Moscow, and within a few months, are able to secure most of the sector's major population centres. With peace returned to the region, the new government declared the foundation of the Spinward Stellar Coalition, and began a sweeping series of reforms to liberalise the economy and promote a new form of democratic rule. With the newfound stability came corporations, who connected the formerly insular territory to the wider Terran Federation, although New Moscow continued to maintain a deliberate distance from Manhattan's rule despite offers to have the Coalition formally join the Terran Federation.

### The Spinward Sector and the Terran Federation
With diplomatic and economic channels now freely open, the Spinward Sector has taken on increasing importance for TerraGov. The Federal Border is now within spitting distance of the Sector itself, with the official Terran settlement of the Permia system, and this has permitted Manhattan to begin to exert true influence over the region, with the eventual goal of integrating the entire Spinward Sector into the Terran fold, as well as securing another route towards the Tiziran Empire. 

The Spinward Sector has taken on a new degree of importance in the last decade or so. The Terran Federal border has progressively crept closer and closer to SSC space, and the two are now close enough that TerraGov has begun to exert true influence over the region, with the eventual goal of integrating the SSC into the wider federation and bringing the entire Spinward Sector into the Terran fold, as well as opening routes to the Tiziran Empire. At the same time, the increased quality of surveys of the region has attracted corporate attention, with the plasma bounty of Indecipheres being the ultimate prize for whichever corporation can take and hold it. While it may be a backwater by most Terran standards, the events unfolding within the Spinward Sector today are hugely important for the future of both the Federal government and the various corporations of human space.

### Population of the Spinward Sector
Given its history with the Red Tomorrow Society, the Spinward Sector is perhaps understandably primarily inhabited by humans, with a relatively large minority population of animalids, and very small communities of Tiziran emigres who fled via the Atiron Route and stayed in the Sector after arriving.

Animalids came to the sector with the various waves of migration during the Soviet period, as the Third Soviet Union had no official state oppression of animalids, and the frontier spirit tends to quash racist beliefs as every hand helps. These sporadic waves evolved into a mature community within the sector, who are culturally integrated in a way that isn't commonly seen in more trailward settlements.
